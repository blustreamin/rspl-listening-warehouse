Data Foundation & Methodology

This section's job is honesty — what the 31,600-record corpus can and cannot support. It turns a wall of caveats into a signal-completeness matrix: for every analytical cut, what share of records actually carries that cue, with a confidence tier. It makes the trust boundary a picture, so no one over-reads a 2.6%-coverage lifestage cut as if it were the whole base.

E-commerce reviews dominate; the Lovingle signal is small but real.

This is observed consumer expression, not a projectable sample. Brand-mention shares are conversation volume, not market share; platform mix reflects the review ecosystem, not purchase reality. Rating- and driver-level reads are robust; demographic, geographic and lifestage cuts are directional — the matrix below shows exactly how thin each is.
