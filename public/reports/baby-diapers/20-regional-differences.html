Regional Differences

India isn't one diaper market. Climate, digital engagement and family structure combine to produce five distinct behavioural signatures — but the online-review lens through which we see them is heavily weighted to one region, and reading the map honestly means saying so first.

Climate and commerce access fracture India into five distinct diaper behaviours.
⚠️ Read this section carefully. Only 1,136 of 31,600 records (3.6%) carry a resolved state — this is the most evidence-constrained section in the report (62% coverage). And Maharashtra alone is 49.3% of that geo-tagged sample, so what reads as the "national" consumer is, in this data, largely the West / Maharashtra voice. East and Central findings are directional only.

Two regions, two realities (real corpus)
▲ West — humid-season problem solved
"Lately my son was having rashes due to the humid season! A friend suggested these and I ordered without expectation — but they surprised me: the amount they soak is unbelievable! Overnight there's no need to change for my 16-month-old. No leakage. Worth every penny." Amazon · Maharashtra, mid-premium — coastal-humidity rash resolved
▼ East — failure tagged to location
"Worst diaper in the world. After 2½ hours it leaked. Fully bad item. From India → West Bengal → Bankura." Amazon · West Bengal (Bankura), Tier-2 — the consumer names the state in the complaint
Brand implication → Treat "national" reads with care — they are Maharashtra-weighted. Message by climate, not by one plan: the North needs a distinct winter leakage-in-cold story that no brand tells; the South needs year-round breathability proof; the West is the sophisticated benchmark to win on softness + rash-safety. East & Central are expansion whitespace — big populations, thin online voice, likely General-Trade-led — but measure before spending, because the review lens under-counts them.
