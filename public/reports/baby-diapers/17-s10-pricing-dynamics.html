Pricing Dynamics

The section carries two things at once — a per-piece price ceiling by segment, and what actually unlocks a higher ceiling. This section leads with a price-ceiling ladder, ranks the ceiling-expanders and price-watch triggers beside it, and — critically — labels the ₹ ranges directional, because they're triangulated from review language + price-value scores, not scraped transaction prices (direct price data was insufficient).

Price-per-diaper math dominates SKU choice — but a higher ceiling is unlocked by proof, not positioning.

Pricing runs on two tracks: a per-piece arithmetic that governs brand and SKU choice in every segment, and a willingness-to-pay ceiling that lifts only on functional proof — overnight dryness and rash-free skin. Ceiling tolerance peaks at Newborn & Crawler (anxiety + overnight salience) and compresses toward potty-training. Promotions load volume; they don't build loyalty.
