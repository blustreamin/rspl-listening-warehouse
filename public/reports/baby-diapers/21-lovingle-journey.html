Brand Spotlight — Lovingle

Lovingle's own conversion story: a small but real trier base that's very satisfied, sitting behind a barrier that is entirely pre-trial. This section draws it as one funnel with a gate — so the single most important fact lands instantly: nothing that blocks Lovingle happens inside the product; it happens before first use.

Rash anxiety blocks trial; softness and value convert triers to loyalists.

The conversion window is narrow but real: price dissatisfaction with incumbents creates trial intent, but rash-safety anxiety — not leakage, not price — is the gate keeping aware non-triers out. Among actual triers (228 mentions, 0.7% SoV), satisfaction on softness and the wetness indicator is disproportionately high. The barrier sits before first use, not inside the product.

Brand implication → Three moves, in order. (1) Attack the gate: credible, visible skin-safety proof (derm/clinical) is worth more than any feature ad — it's the one thing standing between awareness and trial. (2) Fix the real product flags: the negative triers are consistent on fragrance (reads as chemical/rash risk) and band design — both closable, both reinforce the rash barrier if left. (3) Buy familiarity: at 1% SoV, spend on shortlist presence at the trigger moments (incumbent-rash searches, price-curiosity, GT shelf). Get them through the gate and the product keeps them.
