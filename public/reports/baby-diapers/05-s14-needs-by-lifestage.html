Needs by Lifestage

The journey isn't one need getting stronger — it's a sequence of different governing anxieties that reset at each stage, so a brand that wins the newborn can be rejected by the crawler. This section makes that reset the hero: one visual timeline where the governing need, the format shift, the overnight demand and the evidence weight all move together across six stages.

Each lifestage resets the dominant need — forcing product and ritual change.

Skin-safety commands Newborn & Infant; mobility & fit govern the Crawler shift; overnight endurance + size-step confidence define Early Toddler; value calculus drives Middle Toddler churn; potty-training dissolves the category by Late Toddler. The same brand, unchanged, can pass one stage and fail the next.

Brand implication → Lovingle already has genuine advocacy at the Early-Toddler (12–18m) stage, winning on the exact reset that defines it — overnight endurance + absorption.
