Parenting Rituals

The day is built around a handful of caregiving rituals, and the diaper is judged inside each one. This section lays them on a single day-in-the-life ribbon — so two things jump out: night owns the day (the two night rituals are the biggest by far), and the morning skin-inspection is the moment of truth where loyalty is won or lost.

Night-sleep protection dominates every parenting ritual, across all lifestages.

Overnight isn't one concern among many — it's the organising anxiety that binds the day: 81.2% of occasion-coded mentions are night. The fixed sequence is feed → change → sleep, and the diaper must hold 8–10 hours. The morning diaper-off — inspecting sheet & skin — is where a dry, rash-free result renews loyalty and any failure triggers a switch.
