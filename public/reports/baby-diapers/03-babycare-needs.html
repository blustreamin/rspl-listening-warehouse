Category Needs — Babycare (Functional · Emotional · Social)

The wider babycare need-space the diaper decision sits inside. Needs are read across three bands — functional, emotional, social — each rated on how well the category meets it today, with the genuinely unmet gaps promoted to a commercial whitespace panel.

Skin safety and unbroken sleep dominate every babycare conversation.

The need-space is a functional–emotional knot: parents need unbroken overnight sleep and rash-free skin, and the two reinforce each other — a leak or rash at 2am is at once a product failure and a parental-identity failure. Stakes run highest for first-time mothers in nuclear homes, who lack the joint-family buffer and compensate with obsessive review-reading and HCP validation — making peer trust the decisive purchase signal. The category's growth ceiling is less about availability than about trust, transparency and skin-safety credibility.
