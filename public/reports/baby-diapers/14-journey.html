The Decision Journey

Triggers, discovery sources, decision modes, decision-maker, and size selection come together in a 4-stage journey spine, a trust-ranked discovery ladder, and a per-lifestage size table. The core insight: it's not a funnel — it's a rash-anxiety circuit that resets with every skin episode.

Skin-safety anxiety governs first choice; reviews seal the deal.

The mother holds the decisive vote at every stage. Doctors own maximum influence at newborn; online reviews take over by crawler. It's not a linear funnel — a rash episode resets the whole journey, which is why the first diaper experience is the highest-leverage moment in the category.
