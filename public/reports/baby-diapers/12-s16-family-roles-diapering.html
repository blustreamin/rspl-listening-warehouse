Family Roles in Diapering

Who actually changes the diaper — and where the household agrees and fights about it. This section shows the mother's near-total ownership honestly (with a caveat about who writes reviews), then presents the household tensions as a friction ↔ support ledger that makes the real story visible: the extended family both obstructs and enables, and the father is an emerging, celebrated presence.

Mothers own the routine; fathers are visibly stepping in at night.

Daily execution is mother-anchored (92.6% of role-cued mentions). But father involvement — especially overnight — is an emerging, celebrated norm, not an outlier. The sharpest friction is generational (cloth-vs-disposable, change frequency); grandparents create tension and provide relief, making the extended family ambivalent rather than simply adversarial.
