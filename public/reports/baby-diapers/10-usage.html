Night is the dominant occasion; daytime cloth use remains structurally persistent.

Overnight anchors the whole category — it's why disposables get adopted at all. Daytime stays cloth for cost and skin-safety, especially in mass and joint households. Format is developmental: tape at birth → pant by crawler, across every segment.
