Family Roles in Babycare

Who does the daily babycare — and who merely influences it. This section separates executors from influencers (mother is both), shows each role's actual daily tasks, and — importantly — handles the culturally-expected caregivers with no data (grandparents, the maalish wali) honestly rather than inventing a share for them.

Mothers own 93% of caregiving voice; fathers & grandparents are structurally present but silent.

The mother is the undisputed centre — feeding, bathing, skin-care, product choice, from trial to repeat. Everyone else is thin: fathers are an emerging co-participant (a representation gap, not an absence), and the joint-family axis surfaces as normative influence, not daily hands-on care. ◆ The emerging father voice
"As a father, I prioritize my child's comfort and health above all else. When my little one started showing signs of rashes, I knew I had to make a change." Amazon · father, Metro — rare but rising father-authored product advocacy
Brand implication → Trust in Lovingle is built almost entirely through the mother's direct product experience and her peer network — fellow moms are the only influence layer that moves trial. So: (1) make the mother's first experience unimpeachable on skin-safety, and (2) actively seed fellow-mom word-of-mouth (reviews, community, referral) — it's the single highest-leverage external channel. Fathers are a rising secondary voice worth courting, but the mother-plus-peer axis is where the decision is made.
