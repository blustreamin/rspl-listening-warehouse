How parents actually type their way to a diaper. Five intent clusters, ranked by share of categorised search signal, each shown with its real query strings from the corpus — the exact language to bid, title and optimise against. The ~12% long-tail (seasonal, daycare, occasion) that falls below the 5% threshold is dropped, not padded.

Need-state and weight-band signals dominate diaper search language.

Parents navigate the category on four hard anchors — time-on-body (overnight, 12-hour), size by weight-band, brand comparison, and skin safety — with price-value a persistent fifth. Overnight leads by a distance. Crucially, rash-safety search is where the category's most emotionally loaded intent lives — and where Lovingle's conversation is thinnest.
